Organize your repertoire, build gig setlists and practice with OpenSetList — the setlists and chords app for musicians, 100% offline.

Songs & chords
- Full ChordPro editor: lyrics, chords, key, BPM, capo, time signature, duration and a YouTube link.
- One-tap key transposition: raise or lower the whole chart without rewriting it.
- Auto-scroll with speed control to practice at your own pace.
- Hide chords to sing along, resize the lyrics and search within the text.
- Bluetooth pedal support (next/previous/play-pause) so you can keep your instrument in hand.

Setlists for gigs
- Drag to reorder songs and swipe between them in the viewer.
- Set date, venue and time for each gig; the app computes the total set duration.
- Share/export a setlist in OpenSetList (.osl) or JustChords (.chopro) format and import other musicians' setlists.

Organization & backup
- Artists and tags to keep your repertoire organized.
- Full database backup (.db), batch song export/import (JSON) and cloud through the system picker (Google Drive/Dropbox).
- Import from SetList Helper (.db) and JustChords (.chopro).
- Opens .osl/.chopro files received on Android.

Multiplatform & open
- Android, iOS and desktop (Windows/macOS/Linux), open source under the MIT license.
- No accounts, no ads, no data collection. Your data stays on your device.
